07 / SHOW THE MATH

How Saans calculates AQI

CPCB · 2014

Each pollutant gets its own sub-index from a concentration band. The highest valid sub-index becomes the station AQI.

PIECEWISE LINEAR INTERPOLATION
Ip = Ilo + (Cp − BPlo) × Ihi − IloBPhi − BPlo
When can we show an overall AQI?

At least 3 valid pollutants, including PM2.5 or PM10. The CPCB method requires a 24-hour average (or 8-hour for CO and O₃) and 16 hours of data for a sub-index. Saans uses 6 of 8 values for its 8-hour calculations as a documented project choice.

Data credits and limits: OpenAQ · Open-Meteo · NASA FIRMS Method version: cpcb-naqi-2014-v1

ABOUT SAANS

Air, made clear.

Saans is an educational air quality monitor for Ambala and nearby cities. It recomputes CPCB-style AQI from pollutant readings, shows data freshness, and labels possible pollution drivers as likely.

It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Browser location is not stored. If you sign up for alerts, Saans stores only the channel destination, station and threshold.
